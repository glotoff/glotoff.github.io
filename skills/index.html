Photo trip documentary video

photo-trip-documentary-video · added 2026-11-10 · ~50 KB zip

Turn a folder of trip photos and short clips (with GPS) into a 30–35 minute documentary: curated shots, location-based captions, music with ducking, AI narration in two languages (two audio tracks), two subtitle tracks, animated title/chapter/map cards, and a ready-to-upload YouTube package. Includes the pitfalls that cost the most time: spoken TTS prompts, noise bursts and clicks, VLC colour issues, subtitle encodings and YouTube track handling.

How to install

Claude Code: unzip into ~/.claude/skills/ (all projects) or .claude/skills/ (one project) so you get photo-trip-documentary-video/SKILL.md, then ask: “Make a documentary video from my trip photos.”

Other agents / chat tools: unzip anywhere, tell the agent to read SKILL.md and follow it, and give it shell access to the folder. Set TRIP_ROOT to your project folder and GEMINI_API_KEY in the environment.

unzip photo-trip-documentary-video.zip -d ~/.claude/skills/

Needs ffmpeg, Python 3.10+ (Pillow, numpy, imagehash, google-genai), exiftool, and optionally Node + Remotion for animated cards. Music in the original film is Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0), so credit is required.
